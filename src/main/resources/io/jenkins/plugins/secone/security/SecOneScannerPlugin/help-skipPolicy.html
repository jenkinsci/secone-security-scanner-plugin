After an SCA scan the step checks the result against your organisation's SCA policy (Sec1 dashboard, Settings > Configure > SCA policy): severity limits and denied licences. On a breach the build fails, is marked unstable, or continues, as the policy says. Tick this box to skip the policy check for this job.

If no policy is configured the check passes. If the Sec1 server cannot evaluate the policy, the build log shows a warning and the build result is not affected. Not used with asyncScan (fire-and-forget).
